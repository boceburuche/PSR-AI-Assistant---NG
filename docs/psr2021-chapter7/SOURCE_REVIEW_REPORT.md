## Text-preservation checks

The source transcription retains rule numbers, paragraph labels, training-category headings, government sponsorship conditions, benefits and cross-references to Rules 120103, 120227 and 120229. Side-margin headings are stored separately from statutory paragraphs. The chatbot removes extraction-only **[CONTINUATION]** markers and adjacent section headings from individual rule responses but retains the page-by-page reading order and original scanned-page references. Rule 070105 must contain all six lettered categories (a)–(f).

Original page images are archived at `public/psr2021/chapter7/page-68.png` through `page-70.png`. The scanned images remain authoritative if presentation or transcription discrepancies arise. This review is not a certification issued by OHCSF.

**Quality gate:** All 19 rule numbers, three source images and three sections must be visible in the Rulebook. Browser acceptance must confirm source links and answer retrieval for 070105, 070205 and 070304, plus a missing-rule response. Any subsequent source corrections must rerun the tests.
