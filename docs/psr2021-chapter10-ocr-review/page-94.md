SECTION 8.— DISCIPLINARY MEASURES

100801.— The following are the disciplinary measures that may be taken
under and in accordance with these Guidelines :

(i) Dismissal, termination or retirement ;
(ii) Reduction in rank ;

(iii) Reduction in salary ;

(iv) Withholding of increment ;

(v) Deferment of increment ;

(vi) Loss of pay /non- payment for the corresponding days an Officer is
not at his duty post/station without authority ;

(vii) Surcharge ;

(viii) Reprimand ;

(ix) Written warning ; and
(x) Verbal warning.
