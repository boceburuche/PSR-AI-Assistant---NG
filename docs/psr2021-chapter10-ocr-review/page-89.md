Federal Government Public Service Rules
Chapter 10

100418,— Officers shall not, as a general rule, have access to official
and secret records relating personally to themselves.

100419.— No Officer may, on leaving the Public Service take with him

any public record without the written permission of the Permanent Secretary,
Service Policies and Strategies Office, OHCSF.

100420.— Historical manuscripts or other documents of public interest
which may be discovered by any Officer in the course of his official duties
shall not be appropriated to his own use, but their existence must be reported
to Permanent Secretary/ Head of Extra Ministerial Office in order that steps
may be taken for their examination and preservation.

100421,— (i) Except in pursuance of his official duties, no Officer shall,
without the express permission of his Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-
Ministerial Office, whether on duty or on leave of absence :

(a) act as the editor or take part directly or indirectly in the management
of or in any way make financial contribution to any newspaper, magazine
or journal except the following :

(i) Departmental or staff magazine ;
(ii) Professional journal ;
(iii) Publication by voluntary organizations ;

(6) contribute to, whether anonymous or otherwise, or publish in any
newspaper, magazine or periodical or otherwise cause to be published in

any manner anything which may reasonably be regarded as of a political
or administrative nature ;

(c) speak in public or broadcast on any matter which may reasonably
be regarded as of a political or administrative nature ;

(d) allow himself to be interviewed or express any opinion for
publication on any question of a political or administrative nature or on
matters affecting the administration, public policy, defense or military
resources of the Federation or any other Country.

Permanent Secretaries/Heads of Extra-Ministerial Offices.
