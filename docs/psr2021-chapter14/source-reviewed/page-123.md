140144.— Non-accident bonus shall be paid at the rate of N50,000.00
per annum, for drivers who are not involved in an accident or whose
involvement in an accident is not attributed to any faults of their own and is for

drivers with vehicle assigned and have been driving for a period of not less
than six months in a year.
