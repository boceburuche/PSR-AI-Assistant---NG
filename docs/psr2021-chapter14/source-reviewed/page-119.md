140113.— For journeys outside Nigeria, traveling days shall be as
approved by Government from time to time through the issuance of Circular.

140114.— (a) An Officer who is required by Government to proceed to
a foreign country on duty or on an approved course of instruction will be
eligible for a warm clothing allowance as may be specified in the extant
Circular.

(b) The allowance is not payable to an Officer under the following
conditions :

(i) if the duty or course is undertaken during the period of the Officer’s
normal vacation leave spent in a country with a cold or temperate climate ;

(ii) if the duty or course is entered upon as a result of the Officer’s own
application and is taken in conjunction with his normal vacation leave ;
and

(iii) if the duty or course which the Officer is directed to undertake, takes

place earlier than three years from the date on which he last drew warm
clothing allowance.
